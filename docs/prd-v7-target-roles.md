## How this list was built

- **Source:** hh.ru name-search (`search_field=name`, `area=113` = Россия), sorted by publication
  time; experience bands `between1And3` (junior+) and `between3And6` (middle). 237 unique live
  vacancies scanned on 2026-09-19; 20 selected. `[V]`
- **What was excluded:** Senior / Lead / Руководитель / Head / Владелец продукта / pure Product
  Manager roles, system/business/financial analysts, and roles requiring 6+ years (middle+ upper
  band). The target band is **junior+ / middle−** — the deliberate step down (PRD §8).
- **Segments (PRD v7 §1.1, V4):** `1` = product teams with an experimentation culture · `2` =
  retention/growth teams in subscription and fintech.
- **Wedge:** the A/B & Retention specialism (v6 D4). Roles where A/B, retention, funnel or growth
  is in the title or the team are ranked first within each segment.

> **Verify before applying.** Listings expire and requirements change. Open each `hh.ru/vacancy/<id>`
> link, confirm it is still live and that the level is one you will accept, then apply. The list is
> a snapshot, not a standing commitment.

## How to apply (rules)

1. **One tailored cover letter, not a template blast.** Lead with the wedge (A/B + retention),
   then the PhD-in-work-psychology angle as a user-understanding differentiator.
2. **Honest career-change framing stands** (v6 S1.7). No tenure claims — no "4+ years". The public
   portfolio is the evidence; label synthetic data as synthetic (`CONTEXT.md` → Synthetic-as-feature).
3. **Log every application** in `docs/contact-log.md` as a `contact` row with `source=hh`, the
   company, and `segment`.
4. **Advance the stage, don't re-log.** `screening` / `interview` / `final` / `offer` / `rejected`
   are new rows — one per transition, as the log defines.
5. **Compute the gate at 20.** screening-invite rate = applications that produced a `screening`
   row ÷ 20. `≥20% → middle−/junior+ is reachable now`; below 20% → treat middle+ as a 6–12 month
   goal (PRD §5 V1.2). Record the decision in `docs/prd-v7-findings.md`.

## Honest caveats

- Most roles here require "1–3 years" — they are the junior+/middle− band, which is exactly the
  deliberate step down the PRD flags as an owner decision (PRD §8). Applying is the owner's call.
- 20 applications is a small sample; below the 20% gate, treat the result as a strong signal and
  extend to 40 before a final decision (PRD R7.2).
- The roles were harvested programmatically; requirements are not verified beyond title/level/
  location. Read each vacancy before applying.
